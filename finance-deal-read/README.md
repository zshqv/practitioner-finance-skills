# finance:deal-read — Practitioner-Level Deal Commentary for Claude

## The Problem

When you paste deal terms into Claude and ask for a read, it gives you textbook output. Ratios listed. No judgment on whether the price makes sense. No view on where the deal is fragile. No sense of what a buyer actually cares about at the table.

That is not how a senior finance professional reads a deal.

## What It Does

Given any deal input — financials, CIM highlights, term sheet, or a rough set of numbers — this skill produces:

- Entry price assessment: is the multiple defensible vs comps and implied growth?
- Capital structure stress test: can the deal survive a 20% EBITDA decline?
- Value creation thesis read: is the thesis real or financial engineering dressed up as strategy?
- Red flag scan: 15+ financial and structural flags checked against the deal facts
- Overall deal commentary: 2-4 sentences of actual MD-level view, not a list of considerations

## Why This Doesn't Exist Yet

Existing Claude finance skills cover accounting workflows: reconciliation, variance analysis, journal entries. None address how to read a deal situation — which requires anchoring judgment, not just computation.

Claude computes well. It doesn't read deals. This skill fixes that.

## Works Best For

- LBO, M&A, minority stake, recap, and distressed transactions
- Quick IC prep or deal screening
- Training junior analysts to think through deals systematically
- Stress-testing a thesis before a client meeting
